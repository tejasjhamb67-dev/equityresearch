# LIQD Sector Playbooks

Sector-specific lenses that extend the core methodology (`methodology.md`).
When a request names a sector, apply the matching playbook on top of the
standard framework. Each playbook lists the metrics that matter, the macro
drivers to research, and the peer set to screen.

---

## Metals & Mining

**Universe**: Diversified miners (BHP, RIO, GLEN), copper (FCX, SCCO, TECK),
aluminum (AA, CENX), steel (NUE, STLD, CLF, MT), gold/silver (NEM, GOLD, AEM, WPM),
lithium/battery metals (ALB, SQM), uranium (CCJ).

**Macro drivers to research first**
- Spot and futures prices for the relevant metal (LME copper/aluminum/nickel/zinc,
  COMEX gold/silver, iron ore 62% Fe, lithium carbonate) with YTD and YoY moves
- China demand (property, grid, EV output), global PMIs, USD index direction
- Supply: mine disruptions, new project pipeline, treatment/refining charges (TC/RCs),
  exchange inventories (LME/SHFE/COMEX)
- Policy: tariffs, export controls, sanctions, IRA/critical-minerals incentives
- Energy transition demand (copper intensity of EVs/grid, silver in solar)

**Company metrics that matter**
- Production volumes and guidance (tonnes/ounces), realized price vs benchmark
- All-in sustaining cost (AISC) / C1 cash cost and position on the cost curve
- Reserve life, grade trends, capex profile (growth vs sustaining)
- Net debt/EBITDA through the cycle, dividend policy (fixed vs payout-ratio)
- EV/EBITDA and P/NAV vs history and peers; FCF yield at spot vs consensus prices

**Valuation notes**: miners trade on spot-price annualized EBITDA and P/NAV;
run price-deck sensitivity (e.g. ±10% metal price → EBITDA impact). Cyclicals
look cheapest at the top — say where we are in the cycle.

**Risk flags**: single-asset/single-country concentration, resource nationalism,
permitting, water/tailings liabilities, hedge book, by-product credit dependence.

## Energy

**Universe**: Supermajors (XOM, CVX, SHEL, TTE, BP), E&P (COP, EOG, DVN),
services (SLB, HAL, BKR), refiners (MPC, VLO, PSX), midstream (WMB, KMI, ET).

**Drivers**: WTI/Brent strip, Henry Hub/TTF gas, OPEC+ policy, US shale
discipline, refining crack spreads, rig counts.
**Metrics**: FCF breakeven price, reserve replacement ratio, netbacks,
shareholder-return frameworks (base + variable dividend + buyback), leverage.
**Valuation**: EV/DACF, FCF yield at strip; refiners on mid-cycle EPS.

---

## Financials

**Universe**: Money-center banks (JPM, BAC, C, WFC), investment banks (GS, MS),
regionals, insurers, exchanges, asset managers.

**Drivers**: Fed path and yield curve shape, loan growth, credit cycle,
capital-markets activity, regulatory capital rules (Basel endgame).
**Metrics**: NIM, NII guidance, CET1, ROTCE, credit costs/NCOs, book value growth,
efficiency ratio. Valuation: P/TBV vs ROTCE, P/E vs history.

---

## Technology

**Universe**: Mega-cap platforms, semiconductors (NVDA, AMD, AVGO, TSM),
software (MSFT, CRM, NOW), internet (GOOGL, META, AMZN).

**Drivers**: AI capex cycle, cloud growth rates, consumer device cycles,
export controls. **Metrics**: revenue growth durability, NRR for SaaS,
data-center revenue mix, gross-margin trajectory, RPO/backlog.
**Valuation**: EV/S vs growth (rule of 40) for SaaS, P/E vs EPS CAGR for semis.

---

## Healthcare

**Universe**: Large-cap pharma (LLY, NVO, JNJ, MRK, PFE), biotech, med-tech
(ISRG, SYK), managed care (UNH, ELV).

**Drivers**: Pipeline readouts with dates, patent cliffs, FDA calendar,
drug-pricing policy (IRA negotiation rounds), utilization trends.
**Metrics**: pipeline risk-adjusted NPV, LOE exposure %, R&D productivity,
MLR for managed care. **Valuation**: P/E vs growth, pipeline-adjusted DCF.

---

## Consumer

**Universe**: Staples (PG, KO, PEP, COST, WMT), discretionary (HD, NKE, SBUX,
MCD), e-commerce.

**Drivers**: real wage growth, consumer confidence, trade-down behavior,
input-cost inflation, GLP-1 consumption effects.
**Metrics**: same-store sales / comps, volume vs price mix, gross-margin
recovery, inventory health, market-share data. **Valuation**: P/E vs 5-yr
band, organic-growth premium.
